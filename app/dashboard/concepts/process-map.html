From question to memo

Seven windows, four places where you decide. Sketches show layout only; every window extends the Home layout.

You ask a question. The workbench proposes what to research, collects and checks sources, builds findings and writes the output. It stops and asks you only at a gate. Everything it does in between stays visible under the question, so you never have to go looking for progress.

Activity strip

Not a window. It sits directly under the question on every window that belongs to an analysis, and as one line per analysis on Home. Where an AI is producing something in place, such as a scope draft or a memo section, the same running mark appears at that spot and the content fills in as it arrives.

The route

  1. 1

    Home

    Exceptions decided here

    One queue of everything that concerns you: decisions waiting, then your analyses with their live activity line. Select one and its detail opens on the right. This is concept 1, extended.

    On the left
    • Decisions first, in workflow priority: exceptions, intent, publication, governance
    • Then analyses: question, stage, one-line activity, age
    • Filters with counts; “New analysis” at the top
    On the right
    For a decision: the plate, a reason field and the evidence behind it. For an analysis: its activity strip, stage, and links into its windows.
    You
    Decide exceptions with a reason. Open intent or publication reviews. Start a new analysis.
    Exception kinds
    Uncertain relevance, sources that disagree, a contested key judgement, a failure the machine could not recover from

    Selected decision opens the plate on the right.

  2. 2

    New analysis

    You write, nothing runs yet

    Ask the question in your own words and say what you need back. Short, because the next window does the structuring.

    You give
    • Topic or question, free text
    • Output: brief, memo or assessment
    • Audience, geography, time window
    • Profile: cyber, geopolitical, physical security, supply chain
    • Whether to map relevance to Vestas
    Then
    “Propose research plan” sends you to Intent review while the AI drafts it. No sources are collected and no provider is called.

    Anything you leave blank, the AI may fill in, but it is marked as an assumption with its reason on the next window.

    One column, question field first.

  3. 3

    Intent review

    Gate: approve once

    See exactly what will be researched before anything is collected. One approval covers scope, research questions and budget.

    Top
    The question and the activity strip (“AI drafting research questions…” while it fills in)
    Scope
    Purpose, audience, geography, time window, output. Each value marked “Stated by you” or “Assumed”, with the reason.
    Research questions
    Ruled list with rationale. Edit, remove or add. Questions cannot widen the scope.
    Collection budget
    Providers, call limit, time window
    Plate
    “Approve research plan”. Confirming starts collection within this budget. Nothing else changes.

    Assumed values carry the attention mark; a question still drafting shows the AI mark.

  4. Machine works, shown in the activity strip
    1. Collect from Seerist and OSINT
    2. Classify each result
    3. Retrieve source text and anchors
    4. AI checks relevance to each question
    5. Admit by policy
    6. Source assurance

    Uncertain relevance, disagreeing sources and failures appear on Home as decisions. Everything else needs nothing from you.

  5. 4

    Analysis

    Read and explore

    Everything the analysis knows, as it grows. Same list-and-detail layout as Home: select a row, its detail opens on the right.

    Top
    The question and the activity strip
    Evidence tab
    Admitted sources with tier, assurance and the questions they answer. Detail: quote on a rule, anchor, retrieval date. Excluded sources folded below with their reason.
    Findings tab
    Findings grouped by research question, key judgements with support marks, challenge and adjudication, and open gaps
    Decisions
    Any exception for this analysis is listed here and links to Home, where you decide it

    No approvals live here. Changes to the outcome are requested at publication review, so there are only four places where you decide.

    Evidence tab: a source still being checked shows the AI mark in its row.

  6. Machine works, shown in the activity strip
    1. Build claims
    2. Challenge
    3. Adjudicate
    4. Map relevance to Vestas, if asked
    5. Write to the memo standard
    6. Independent verification

    A contested key judgement becomes a decision on Home. When verification finishes, the strip says the output is ready for your review.

  7. 5

    Publication review

    Gate: approve, return or reject

    Read the output as its reader will, and open the evidence behind any claim. This is concept 2.

    Top
    The question, the activity strip, and the plate with blockers from verification
    Left
    The memo, every claim clickable, with its support mark
    Right
    Trace for the selected claim: quotes, sources, tier, assurance, contradicting evidence, challenge
    Sampled checks
    Five claim-to-source rows chosen at random. You record whether each source supports its claim.
    You
    Approve, reject, or return to the stage that owns the problem with a note

    Memo left, trace right; approval stays closed while blockers remain.

  8. 6

    Output

    Published

    The finished brief, memo or assessment, read cleanly, with everything behind it one step away.

    Reader
    Bottom line, key judgements, implications, indicators to watch, sources. Limitations stated, including “support check: model-only, unvalidated” until calibration is approved.
    Actions
    Export to PDF or Word, copy a link, start a follow-up analysis from this one
    Audit trail
    Every decision with its reason and who made it, and the versions of policies, standards and context used
    Versions
    Earlier versions and what changed

    Reading width for the document, actions and audit in a narrow margin.

  1. 7

    Governance

    Gate, on its own cadence

    The rules every analysis runs under. Changed rarely and never per memo. Pending approvals also appear on Home.

    Left
    Memo standard, organisational context packs, source assurance policies, outlet reliability tables, each with its current version
    Right
    The proposed version with a change list against the current one, and which analyses would use it
    You
    Approve or reject a new version. Analyses record the exact versions they used, so nothing changes under a finished output.

    Same list-and-detail pattern; the plate opens for a pending version.
